MIGRIZO CRM — PHASE 2A: PHONE AUDIT + NEW CONVERSATION
=======================================================

WHAT THIS ADDS
--------------
1. A reachability audit of every lead in your CRM — how many you can
   actually message on WhatsApp, and why the rest you cannot.
2. A "New conversation" button in the WhatsApp inbox: pick a lead,
   pick an approved template, send. The inbox was reply-only until
   now; this is what makes it an outbound channel.

THE AUDIT — READ THIS BEFORE BUILDING SEQUENCES
-----------------------------------------------
The strip along the top of the New Conversation screen is the answer
to "is outbound worth it at scale?":

  reachable        valid number, not opted out, no thread yet
  already talking  valid number, conversation already exists
  no number        phone field is empty
  bad number       phone field has something, but not a valid
                   WhatsApp number (wrong digit count, junk text)
  opted out        replied STOP/NO — never messaged again, ever

Every lead lands in exactly one bucket and the buckets sum to your
total. If they ever stop summing, the classification has a hole.

You can also run it directly:

  select jsonb_pretty(public.whatsapp_phone_audit('<your-workspace-id>'));

That returns the same counts plus:
  reachable_by_stage   is your outbound audience actually cold leads,
                       or people already deep in the pipeline?
  unusable_examples    up to 25 of the rejected numbers. This is the
                       actionable part — most are one missing digit
                       or a stray country code, and seeing them is
                       what makes them fixable.

Why this matters: a 5-message sequence across 2,000 leads and across
300 leads are different businesses. Meta charges per conversation and
quality rating punishes sending to numbers that are not on WhatsApp.
Know the real number before committing to the sequence engine.

"Reachable" here means exactly what the send route accepts — both use
whatsapp_normalize_phone() from migration 040. There is deliberately
no second definition of "valid" that could drift from the first.


NEW CONVERSATION — HOW IT BEHAVES
---------------------------------
Two steps: who, then what.

STEP 1, pick a lead. Search by name, number or email. Filter by
stage. "Sendable only" is on by default.

  Design decision: unsendable leads are SHOWN, greyed out, with the
  reason — not hidden. If they were hidden, searching for a client and
  getting nothing would mean either "not in your CRM" or "no valid
  number" or "opted out" — three different problems that look
  identical. A greyed row saying "Opted out" answers it immediately.

STEP 2, pick what to send.

  - Only APPROVED templates are listed. Draft ones are excluded
    because selecting one would just produce a Meta rejection at send
    time. All 11 of yours are draft right now, so this list will be
    empty with an explanatory note until Meta approves them.

  - "Free-form message" appears ONLY if that lead already messaged
    you and the 24-hour window is still open. Meta does not allow
    opening a cold conversation with free text. The screen states
    which of the two reasons applies rather than just disabling the
    option.

  - Variables prefill: {{1}} is the lead's first name, matching the
    convention across all 11 templates. Titles are stripped, so
    "Dr. Vikram Rao" gives "Vikram", not "Dr.".

  - Live preview shows the exact message that lands on their phone.

Escape steps back from step 2 to step 1 before it closes the dialog —
losing a filled-in template because you wanted to change the
recipient would be maddening.

After sending, the list refreshes and the new thread opens
automatically.
