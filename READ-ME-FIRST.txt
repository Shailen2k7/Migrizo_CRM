Everything outstanding in the module is now built. No automation was

INSTALL
  1. Drag app/, components/, lib/, supabase/ into the repo root,
     replacing files. Push.
  2. Supabase → SQL Editor → run:
       supabase/migrations/048_whatsapp_media.sql
     Safe to run twice. It also creates the private storage bucket.
  3. Hard-refresh the CRM.

WHAT SHIPPED

1 · SPEED — the headline
    Clicking a conversation used to clear the thread, show a spinner
    and wait for a round trip. Every single click.

    Now threads live in a module-level cache:
      • a visited conversation paints SYNCHRONOUSLY — zero frames
        of blank, no spinner at all
      • the refresh happens behind you and only repaints if
        something actually changed
      • hovering a row starts its fetch before you click
      • the top 8 conversations are prefetched right after the list
        loads, so even the FIRST click is usually instant
      • hover + click + realtime share ONE request, never three
      • read receipts are fire-and-forget — nobody waits on them
      • the cache is LRU-bounded at 60 threads

    Opening 5 conversations twice: 10 round trips before, 5 now,
    and the 5 you feel are the prefetched ones you never see.

2 · ATTACHMENTS — send and receive, end to end
    • Paperclip in the composer, or DRAG A FILE onto the thread
    • Images, PDFs, audio, video — rendered properly in the bubble,
      not as "[Document]"
    • Inbound files are DOWNLOADED into our own storage, not left
      as an Interakt link that expires. A lead's CV stays readable
      forever instead of rotting into a 404.
    • Size limits enforced per type; executables refused

    SECURITY: the bucket is PRIVATE. The browser never gets a
    storage URL — every image and download goes through
    /api/whatsapp/media/<id>, which checks your session and your
    workspace first. These are CVs and passports; a shareable
    public link was not an acceptable design.

3 · POP-OUT CHAT WINDOW
    Double-click any conversation — it opens as its own clean
    window at /wa/<id>. No sidebar, no chrome, pure chat. There is
    also a pop-out button in the conversation header.
    It shares the thread cache, so it opens already painted.

4 · SIDEBAR COLLAPSE
    Toggle at the top of the CRM menu, or press \ anywhere.
    Collapses to a 68px icon rail; the choice is remembered.
    WhatsApp unread shows as a live count when expanded and a green
    dot on the icon when collapsed — realtime, so it moves the
    moment a lead replies.

5 · START / STOP FROM THE CHAT WINDOW
    Pause / Resume / Stop now sit in the conversation header, not
    only in the side panel — you can stop a sequence from the
    window where you noticed the problem.

6 · SAVED REPLIES + EMOJI
    Four saved replies seeded (fees, docs, timeline, book a call),
    editable in the database. Emoji picker in the composer.

7 · DESIGN
    Avatars in the thread rows, tightened spacing and hover states,
    honest empty and loading states, refined attachment cards.
