MIGRIZO — INBOX RESKIN (the approved "Kinetic Clarity" design)
==============================================================

WHAT THIS IS
  The WhatsApp Inbox and contact panel rebuilt to the design you
  approved (the Stitch/Claude HTML). Pixel geometry, colours and
  type scale come straight from that file. Every existing function
  is preserved — nothing about sending, sequences, caps, windows
  or opt-outs changed.

WHAT MATCHES THE MOCK
  Top bar     wordmark "WhatsApp | Inbox", centred segmented tabs
              (active = white + shadow), white presence pill.
  Threads     minimal two-line rows, 3px green spine + soft green
              fill on the selected row, 13.5px names, 12px preview.
  Chat        56px header with the indigo avatar; TODAY divider in
              letterspaced caps; asymmetric bubble corners; outbound
              #DCF6D4, inbound white+hairline; the MARKETING
              micro-tag; timestamps under each group.
  Composer    boxed field with the tinted control bar, attach/emoji/
              template icons, dark-navy Send with the send icon.
  Panel       #FAFBFC column of white cards: indigo hero, dark tab
              underline, ACTIVE SEQUENCE card with progress bar,
              DETAILS stacked label/value, TAGS.

WHAT WAS KEPT THAT THE MOCK DIDN'T SHOW (function first)
  Filter chips (All/Unread/Needs reply/Open/Failed) · unread badge
  and bold names · amber needs-reply / red opted-out dots · window
  countdown on rows, in the header pill and as its own card ·
  read/delivered ticks · failed-message state inside the bubble ·
  Pause/Resume/Stop wired to the real enrollment · 24-hour window
  card · suppressed banner · dry-run notice · column collapse and
  keyboard shortcuts.

INSTALL
  Drag app/ and components/ into the repo root, replace, push.
  No migration, no env change — this is presentation only.
