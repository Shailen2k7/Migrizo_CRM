  AUTOMATIC DAILY ENROLMENT
════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

WHAT IT DOES

A toggle and two numbers on the Automation tab: cold per day and hot
per day. Switch it on and the system enrols that many fresh leads
every day by itself, oldest untouched first, never repeating anyone.
You stop pressing Enrol.

Hot is a separate number on purpose. Hot leads are scarcer and their
sequence is shorter, five emails over twenty days against six over
thirty. One shared number would either burn through the hot pool or
starve the cold one.

It runs once per Indian calendar day, driven by the same thirty
minute clock that sends the emails. If the clock misses a run the
next one catches up. If it fires ten times in an hour, only the
first enrols anything.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
THE NUMBER THAT SURPRISES PEOPLE
──────────────────────────────────────────────
Enrolling 50 leads a day does NOT send 50 emails a day.

Every lead receives one email per step. The cold sequence has six
steps, so at a steady rate you settle at rate x 6. Fifty cold a day
becomes about 300 emails a day once every stage is running.

Your cap starts at 30 and ramps to 180 over four weeks, and the cap
always wins, so numbers set too high just make new leads queue up
and start late.

The card does this arithmetic for you and turns red with a suggested
pair of numbers if you go over. Start around 20 cold and 5 hot while
the cap is still low, then raise both as it ramps.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
STEP 3 · SWITCH IT ON
──────────────────────────────────────────────
Campaigns, Automation tab, "Top up every day". Set your two numbers,
press Save numbers, then flip the toggle. First batch goes in at the
next tick, within thirty minutes.

──────────────────────────────────────────────
TESTED
──────────────────────────────────────────────
Enrols exactly the configured numbers; a second run the same day
adds nothing; the next day adds a fresh batch; no lead is ever
enrolled twice; junk and suppressed leads are never touched; asking
for more leads than exist drains the pool without erroring; running
against an empty pool is harmless; the over-cap warning fires at the
right threshold.
