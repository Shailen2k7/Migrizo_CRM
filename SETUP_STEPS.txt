STEP 3 · OPEN IT
  Sidebar → Campaigns (only YOU see anything — you are the workspace owner).
  On first open the 9 Global Talent Visa templates seed themselves:
  4 cold + 5 hot, grouped in the Templates tab.

WHO CAN USE IT
  • You (workspace owner / super admin): everything.
  • Everyone else: a "restricted" screen — even other admins.
  • Access tab (owner only): grant campaign rights to any member with one
    click; revoke the same way. Granted members get everything except the
    Access tab itself.

THE FOUR TABS
  Send      → pick recipients → pick template → edit subject + content →
              mandatory preview → launch. Sending is blocked while any
              [square-bracket] placeholder remains in the content.
  Templates → all 9 GTV templates + your custom ones. Edit name, subject and
              content; add new; delete. Content-only editing: the frame
              (logo, GTV badge, Shailen's signature, booking CTA, WhatsApp,
              unsubscribe button) is applied automatically at send time so
              every email keeps an identical format.
  History   → every campaign with live progress. Pause (queue holds),
              Resume, Stop (queue cancelled permanently).
  Access    → grant/revoke campaign rights (owner only).

EVERY EMAIL AUTOMATICALLY CARRIES
  • Migrizo logo header — identical to your SLA/roadmap emails
  • "GLOBAL TALENT VISA · UNITED KINGDOM" badge before any content, so the
    reader knows exactly what the email is about
  • Shailen's plain text signature: name, Lead Consultant title, Migrizo,
    Migrizo Ventures Pvt Ltd., phone, website, email
  • "Book a 1-to-1 call →" button → https://crm.migrizo.com/book/shailen
  • WhatsApp line → +44 7887 348822
  • Navy footer with a one-click Unsubscribe BUTTON (plus the RFC-8058
    List-Unsubscribe headers Gmail requires)

SAFETY BUILT IN
  • Unsubscribed / bounced addresses are removed at queue time AND re-checked
    at send time (covers people who unsubscribe after being queued).
  • Paused/stopped campaigns hold their queue at the sending layer.
  • Preview file included: campaign-email-preview.html — open it to see
    exactly what a recipient receives.
